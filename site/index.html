The ball moves when the skills move

Standard path is red to 8, orange 8–10, green 9–10, yellow 11+. Teddy is already ahead of it on green, so there is nothing to rush. The yellow ball bounces above a 7-year-old's shoulder on a full court and trains late, defensive contact; green is what protects the form that gets him to 20.

Load rule. The home program budgets high-intent efforts (all-out sprints, max jumps, max throws), not minutes: 40 per week, 20 in Trials weeks. Saturday alone costs 50 to 70, so Sunday and Monday home work has zero, Friday at most 5, and Wednesday carries the rest. Stop any set on form collapse, heel or knee pain, or the third slower rep in a row.
