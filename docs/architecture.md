# Teddy PE: Program Architecture (Sep 2026 – Aug 2027)

This is the source document for the year. Monthly views and weekly daily cards are regenerated from it. Change this doc first, then regenerate.

## Athlete

- Teddy, 7, homeschooled. Only child. Reads well; can follow his own daily card.
- Very advanced tennis player for his age. Currently on **green** ball.
- Baseline (Sep 2026): excellent bike rider, swims, catches a tennis ball out of the air, throws overhand without full rotation, no cartwheel yet.
- Loves nearly everything, especially doing it with Dad.
- Coach and training partner: Jeff. Running capacity limited in fall, ramping through winter. Fall sessions are designed so Jeff feeds, times, demonstrates and competes from a fixed position; chase and race games with Jeff phase in during the Coyote block.
- Location: Manhattan through October; West Chester, PA from November. Assume the Riverside Park traveling rings, the skate park, courts, wall and fields all remain available through the year. The Fox block is still kept equipment-light and indoor-friendly so the move itself costs nothing.

## North star

Compete at the highest levels of tennis at 20. Not precocious at 8.

What that implies for a 7 year old, and what this program does about it:
- Broad sampling until roughly 12 (the current five-sport roster is an asset here), with tennis hours growing gradually rather than front-loaded.
- Stroke and footwork form protected over results. Ball progression gated on skill, never on age or calendar.
- The coordination window (roughly 6 to 12) spent on the widest possible movement vocabulary: tumbling, rings, skateboard, throwing both arms, rhythm.
- Strength built through bodyweight, hanging, climbing and med ball. No loaded lifting until growth and technique justify it.
- Competition framed as a skill to practice (point construction, scoring, resetting after errors), not a verdict. Handicapped matches vs Dad are the main vehicle.
- Recovery treated as training: Saturday is enormous, so Sunday and Monday home work is skill only, and every 8th week is Trials at half volume.

## Load rule: the high-intent effort budget

Hours are a poor proxy for load at this age. The program budgets **high-intent efforts** (HIE): all-out sprints over 10m, maximal jumps and hops, maximal throws, hard cuts under fatigue.

- Weekly HIE cap for the home program: **40** in a normal week, **20** in a Trials/deload week.
- Saturday alone (soccer, lacrosse, tennis) is assumed to cost 50-70 HIE. That is why Sunday and Monday home sessions contain zero HIE and Friday contains at most 5.
- Wednesday carries most of the home budget (20-30). Thursday carries 5-10.
- Never two consecutive high-impact days from the home program.
- Stop signals for any session: form collapses, heel or knee pain, or the third consecutive slower rep. Quality collapses long before the kid wants to stop.

Jeff has chosen to treat soccer, lacrosse and swim as play rather than training load. The program design does not rely on that assumption: the intensity waving above protects Teddy either way.

## Seven key areas (rows of the year visual)

1. **Speed & Acceleration**: posture, braking, first step, acceleration, max velocity, speed under chaos.
2. **Power & Landing**: landing mechanics first, then hops, jumps, bounds, plyos, reactive power.
3. **Coordination & Balance**: tumbling (cartwheel is the year's headline skill), rhythm, footwork, jump rope, skateboard, balance.
4. **Strength & Resilience**: hanging, climbing, pulling, crawling, carrying, rotational strength, durability.
5. **Throw & Catch**: overhand rotation (both arms), catching under pressure, throwing accuracy and distance.
6. **Tennis**: four strands running all year: **Form** (forehand/backhand), **Footwork**, **Ball progression** (gated, see below), **Match play**.
7. **Play & Compete**: games, head-to-head vs Dad with shrinking handicaps, tournaments, free play.

## Six blocks, six ranks

Each block is 8 weeks. Week 8 of every block is **Trials**: volume drops by half, Teddy attempts the patches he has not yet earned, and he ranks up. Ranking up requires **five of seven patches** (so one weak area never blocks progress, but he can't skip two).

| Rank | Dates | Focus | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cub** | Sep 14 – Nov 8 | Landing, braking, body control, cartwheel foundations, stroke form | Teach stopping before going. Lowest injury debt. Works anywhere, survives the move prep. |
| **Fox** | Nov 9 – Jan 3 | Tumbling, hanging, throwing rotation, jump rope, footwork without a ball | The move window. Indoor-friendly so the move costs nothing. Thanksgiving and Christmas weeks are light by design. |
| **Coyote** | Jan 4 – Feb 28 | Strength, rotational power, footwork patterns, rally consistency | Winter. Build the engine while it is cold. Jeff returns to chase games. |
| **Wolf** | Mar 1 – Apr 25 | Sprint mechanics, acceleration, hills, plyos, serve | Peak speed block. Outdoors, fresh tissue, Jeff running. |
| **Puma** | Apr 26 – Jun 20 | Agility under decision, point construction, competition | Apply everything under chaos. Match play block. |
| **Cheetah** | Jun 21 – Aug 15 | Adventure, water, bike, highest free play, final retest | The loosest block of the year on purpose. |

Aug 16 – Sep 12: transition. No program. Next year's architecture written in this window.

### Block content by area

**Speed & Acceleration**
- Cub: Athletic stance, quiet 3-step stops, split step, reaction to Dad's signal. Chase games with Dad stationary.
- Fox: Sprint posture indoors: wall drill (6-8 reps, never 20), fast feet, hallway reaction starts.
- Coyote: Acceleration mechanics: falling starts, 5-10m bursts, band-resisted starts, first-step games.
- Wolf: Max velocity: 20-30m flys, hill sprints (4-6 reps), timed gates, PR chasing.
- Puma: Speed under chaos: tag variants, ball chase, sprint-then-hit, multi-directional starts.
- Cheetah: Free speed: races on grass and sand, bike sprints, retest.

**Power & Landing**
- Cub: Stick landings from ground, curb, 30cm box. Single-leg stick. "Silent landing" scoring.
- Fox: Jump rope streaks, pogo hops, low rhythm hops. No max jumps.
- Coyote: Bilateral power: broad jumps, low box jumps, med ball slams.
- Wolf: Plyo and hills: hill jumps and backpedals, bounding, mini-hurdle hops, single-leg hops.
- Puma: Reactive power: jump-and-catch, rebound jumps, sport jumps (rebounds, headers).
- Cheetah: Play power: pool jumps, long-jump pit, trampoline if available.

**Coordination & Balance**
- Cub: Cartwheel steps 1-3 (bunny hops over a line, hand-hand-foot-foot, lunge entry). Wall handstand prep (donkey kicks). Single-leg balance 30s. Skateboard.
- Fox: Cartwheel steps 4-5 (straight legs, from standing). Forward and backward rolls. Wall handstand holds. Jump rope singles to 30.
- Coyote: Footwork: agility ladder, crossover steps, jump rope tricks, cartwheel both sides.
- Wolf: Rhythm and bounding: skips, gallops, carioca, A-skips, B-skips.
- Puma: Agility with decision: cone drills on Dad's call, mirror drills, sport footwork.
- Cheetah: Adventure balance: skateboard, bike skills, log walks, slackline, water.

**Strength & Resilience**
- Cub: Dead hangs, traveling rings intro (Riverside), animal walk medleys.
- Fox: Rings passes to 5 and back, hanging knee raises, monkey bars. Hollow holds, crab and bear walks.
- Coyote: Rotational strength and carries: med ball rotations, suitcase carries, push-up progressions, climbing.
- Wolf: Elastic strength: hill backpedals, single-leg work, balance-board squats and lunges with tennis ball.
- Puma: Durability: maintain, circuits, partner pushing and pulling games.
- Cheetah: Climb and swim: playground climbing, tree and rock climbing where safe, swimming as strength.

**Throw & Catch**
- Cub: Step-and-throw (opposite foot forward), both arms, at a wall target.
- Fox: Hip turn: add trunk rotation, med ball hip throws, non-dominant throwing volume.
- Coyote: Full throwing sequence (step, hip, shoulder, arm). Distance and accuracy games.
- Wolf: Overhead throwing into the tennis serve pattern. Catching under pressure (tennis ball from bounce, high pop-ups).
- Puma: Throwing accuracy competitions, lacrosse and basketball application, one-hand catches.
- Cheetah: Sport sampling: baseball catch, frisbee, water tosses.

**Tennis** (four strands)
- Cub: *Form*: unit turn, contact in front, finish over the shoulder; shadow swings, side feeds, drop feeds. *Footwork*: ready position, split step on Dad's clap, shuffle recovery. *Ball*: current ball; wall rally. *Match*: cooperative rally counting.
- Fox: *Form*: both wings equal volume; med ball forehand/backhand rotation; one-hand vs two-hand backhand settled. *Footwork*: adjust steps and recovery shuffle without a ball; footwork patterns in the hallway. *Ball*: wall rally with the next ball up. *Match*: mini-tennis points.
- Coyote: *Form*: open-stance forehand; consistent low-to-high path. *Footwork*: crossover recovery step, first step to the ball. *Ball*: cooperative rally on the next ball, depth targets, 20-ball rallies. *Match*: directional control games (cross-court, down the line).
- Wolf: *Form*: serve pattern from overhead throw; volley (charge, split, catch). *Footwork*: approach footwork, split step before volley. *Ball*: gate check. *Match*: serve + 1, attack the short ball.
- Puma: *Form*: form under pace, Dad hits harder feeds. *Footwork*: recovery under match tempo. *Ball*: gate check; controlled yellow exposure if gates are met. *Match*: point construction, scoring, tiebreaks, tournament vs Dad, patterns (cross-court default, attack short, serve + 1).
- Cheetah: *Form*: maintain. *Ball*: whatever the gates allow. *Match*: fun matches, local kids, retest rally counts.

**Play & Compete**
- Cub: Games with Dad stationary: wall ball, HORSE, roll-out sharks and minnows, tag with a "base".
- Fox: Indoor games. Rings and skate park stay in the rotation through the move. Holiday weeks light.
- Coyote: Winter games, basketball, indoor tennis, first chase games with Dad.
- Wolf: Races and timed challenges. Handicap vs Dad narrows.
- Puma: Tournaments and head-to-head vs Dad. Tennis matches.
- Cheetah: Free play, bike adventures, pool, minimal structure.

## Tennis ball progression: gates, not dates

Standard developmental path: red to age 8, orange 8-10, green 9-10, yellow 11+. Teddy is on green at 7, already ahead of it. Yellow on a full court at 7 bounces above his shoulder, which trains late, high, defensive contact and undoes the form work. So the ball moves when the skills move.

- **Orange → Green**: cleared. Teddy is on green.
- **Green → Controlled yellow exposure** (hand feeds, short court, wall only). **This is the active gate.** Requires: 20-ball green rally on the full court; handles green high bounce with spacing (not jammed) on 8 of 10; serve in 6 of 10; can hit a cross-court pattern on request.
- **Yellow full-court match play**: held until controlled exposure has been clean for a full block and his coach agrees. Even then, green stays the default rally ball for form work this year. Reassess in September 2027.

Gate checks happen in Trials weeks.

## Test battery (baseline Sep 15-17, retest Dec 7-11, Mar 1-5, Jun 14-18, Aug 9-13)

| # | Test | Protocol | Feeds area |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20m sprint | Standing start, 2-3 trials, full rest, phone timer. Best. | Speed |
| 2 | Standing broad jump | 3 trials, measure heel. Best. Must stick the landing to count. | Power & Landing |
| 3 | Single-leg hop, each leg | 2 trials each, distance. Record both; a gap over 15% is a flag. | Power & Landing |
| 4 | Overhand throw, each arm | Tennis ball, 3 throws each arm, distance. Best each. | Throw & Catch |
| 5 | Dead hang | Best of 2, seconds. | Strength |
| 6 | Line-touch agility | 5m out, touch, 5m back, touch, 5m out. 2 trials. Best. | Speed / Coordination |
| 7 | Jump rope singles | Consecutive, best of 3. | Coordination |
| 8 | Tennis rally count | Cooperative rally with Dad, current ball, best of 3. | Tennis |

Skill unlocks (pass/fail, checked in Trials): cartwheel over a line; cartwheel both sides; forward roll to standing; wall handstand 10s; 30s single-leg balance eyes closed; 20m backward run; 5 traveling-rings passes; split step on 10 of 10 signals; serve in 6 of 10.

## Weekly structure

Every week has **one theme** and **three or four sub-targets** across areas, always including one tennis sub-target. Every week has a **Challenge of the Week** that is a playful, scaled version of one battery test, attempted Monday (or Tuesday) and again Friday so Teddy sees a number move inside a single week.

### Cub block weekly themes (Sep 14 – Nov 8)

| Wk | Dates | Theme | Sub-targets | Challenge of the week |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sep 14-20 | **Baseline & Land** | Full test battery; athletic stance and stick landing from the ground; cartwheel step 1 (bunny hops); split step on Dad's clap | Silent Landings: 10 jumps off a step, count the silent ones |

### Cub patches (need 5 of 7 to become a Fox)

- **Speed**: quiet 3-step stop from a jog, 5 in a row; split step on 10 of 10 signals.
- **Power & Landing**: 10 silent stick landings in a row from a 30cm box; stuck broad jump.
- **Coordination & Balance**: cartwheel over a line, hands and feet on the line (form, not yet straight legs); 30s single-leg balance each leg.
- **Strength**: 20s dead hang; 5 traveling-rings passes with spot, or bear walk 20m without knees touching.
- **Throw & Catch**: step-and-throw with the opposite foot, 10 of 10, both arms.
- **Tennis**: 15-ball cooperative rally; forehand and backhand from drop feed with unit turn and finish over the shoulder, 8 of 10.
- **Play & Compete**: complete 6 of 8 Challenge-of-the-Week checks and show up to Trials.

## Daily card template

Full session (Wed/Thu/Fri, and shorter Tue):
1. **Wake Up** (8-12 min): animal walk medley, skips, crawls, mobility. Never skipped.
2. **New Thing** (15-20): the week's skill unlock. Highest attention, lowest fatigue, so it goes early.
3. **Fast or Strong** (12-15): speed/power on Wed, strength on Tue/Thu. Low volume, full rest, disguised as a game.
4. **Tennis or Throw** (15-30): heaviest on Thu.
5. **Challenge of the Day** (5-8): one scored thing, vs a PR or vs Dad with a handicap.
6. **Play** (20-40): a game Teddy picks or a sport on the field/court.
7. **Shake Out** (5): walk, calves, breathing, one thing he did well today.

Quick card (Sun, and Mon after gym): Wake Up (5) → New Thing (10) → Challenge (5) → Review (5).

## Motivation system

Rank card with seven patch slots per rank. Patches earned in Trials weeks (early earning allowed if he clearly has it). Rank-up ceremony every 8th Sunday after tennis. Challenge of the Week gets a Monday number and a Friday number on a visible chart. Head-to-head vs Dad uses explicit handicaps that shrink each block as Jeff's running returns.

## Regeneration instructions (for future sessions)

To generate a month: take the block, list the week themes and sub-targets for that month, map to the fixed day roles, keep HIE budget, place Trials/deload and test weeks. To generate a week of daily cards: use the daily card template, place the week's New Thing in every full session, distribute the sub-targets across Wed/Thu/Fri with tennis heaviest Thursday, and write the Challenge of the Week into Monday/Tuesday and Friday.
