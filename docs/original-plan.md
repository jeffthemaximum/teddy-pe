## What changed and why

- **The speed workout was cut to a third of its volume.** As written it is 35+ high-intent efforts, an adult sprint session. A 7 year old produces 10 to 14 quality max efforts before mechanics degrade. Wall drill is 6 to 8 reps; starts are 6 to 10 per session; all of it disguised as a game.
- **Bulgarian jumps and hill work moved to the Wolf block (Mar–Apr)** when tissue is fresh, Jeff is running again, and the landing mechanics from the Cub block are in place.
- **The tennis-ball-on-a-board strength work was kept nearly intact.** It is excellent motor learning for this age and appears throughout Strength & Resilience.
- **Animal walks became the daily Wake Up.** Every session starts with them.
- **Added what was missing:** tumbling and the cartwheel, hanging and rings, throwing rotation with both arms, deliberate deloading, a test battery, and a rank system.
