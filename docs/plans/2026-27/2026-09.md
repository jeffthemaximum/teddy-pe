# Cub block · Weeks 1–3 (Sep 14 – Oct 4)

## Week 1: Baseline & Land (Sep 14–20)

Sub-targets: Full test battery recorded, all 10 plus height; Athletic stance and stick landing from the ground; Cartwheel step 1: bunny hops over a line; Tennis: split step on Dad's clap; Basketball: 200 stationary dribbles a session, eyes up; Soccer: first touch off the wall, both feet; keeper W-catch

Challenge of the week: Silent Landings. 10 jumps off a step, count the silent ones. Monday number, Friday number.

High-intent efforts: 28 of 40.

### Mon 2026-09-14 · Floor Day · Land Like a Cat (60–75 min, high-intent efforts: 0)

- **Wake Up** (8): Animal walk medley: bear, crab, monkey, flamingo, giraffe, 10m each. Then 10 skips.
- **New Thing** (12): Stick landing from the ground. Jump up, land in cat position: feet quiet, knees over toes, chest up, hands out front. Freeze 3 seconds. 10 reps, Dad scores each 1 to 3 for quiet. Then 10 from a curb or bottom step.
- **Cartwheel step 1** (10): Bunny hops over a chalk line. Hands on the line, kick both feet over to the other side, land feet together. 10 each direction. Arms straight, look at your hands.
- **Basketball: Handle** (20): Stationary handling, eyes up on Dad's fingers (he holds up numbers, Teddy calls them). Pound dribble 25 each hand, crossovers 25, figure 8 through the legs 10 each way, then again. About 200 dribbles. Ball below the waist. Eyes up, ball low.
- **Challenge [challenge]** (7): Silent Landings. 10 jumps off the step. Count how many make no sound. Write the number on the chart. Again Friday.
- **Champion's Log** (5): First entry. One line: best thing today. Teddy writes it himself.
- **Play** (10): Skateboard cruise or bike. Nothing timed.

Dad notes: Gym already loaded him. Zero sprinting and zero jumping for distance today. The basketball block is volume, not intensity: 200 dribbles is the target, and losing the ball is fine, looking down is the thing to correct. Watch the knees on landing: if they cave inward, lower the step.

### Tue 2026-09-15 · Rings Day · Test Day 1: Hands & Arms (75–90 min, high-intent efforts: 6)

- **Wake Up** (10): Skips, big and small arm circles, bear crawl 2×10m, inchworms ×5.
- **Test: Height [battery]** (3): Against a wall, shoes off, heels down. Record cm. Repeat at every retest.
- **Test: Dead hang [battery]** (5): Best of 2, 2 min rest between. Straight arms, thumbs around the bar. Record seconds.
- **Test: Overhand throw [battery]** (10): Tennis ball, from a step. 3 throws right, 3 throws left. Mark the best with a cone, pace it. Record both arms.
- **Test: Jump rope [battery]** (8): Consecutive singles without a miss. Best of 3. Record.
- **Single-leg balance** (5): Each leg, eyes open, arms out, up to 60s. Record each leg (skill unlock, not a battery item).
- **Rings** (15): Riverside traveling rings. Hang and swing ×5. Then reach for the next ring with Dad spotting the hips. Goal: 3 passes. If he gets 3, try for 5.
- **Soccer: Touch** (15): At a wall. Pass, receive, pass: 25 with the right foot, 25 with the left. Then toe taps 30s, tick-tocks 30s, sole rolls 30s, twice through. About 300 touches. Soft foot, look up after the touch.
- **Play** (15): Basketball court. HORSE, Dad shoots from one spot.

Dad notes: Tests before rings, while he is fresh. Full rest between throws; these are max efforts. Cheer the effort, record the number without commentary. The non-dominant arm and the left foot will look bad. That is the point: they are what will move most this year. Swim class after is recovery, add nothing.

### Wed 2026-09-16 · Fast Day · Test Day 2: Fast & Far (100–120 min, high-intent efforts: 15)

- **Wake Up** (12): A-march 2×15m, skips 2×15m, gallops each lead 2×15m, high knees 2×10m. Then 3 build-up runs over 20m at 60, 70, 80%.
- **Test: 20m sprint [battery]** (10): Standing start behind a line, Dad at 20m with the phone timer: ready, set, go. 3 trials, walk-back rest of 2 min. Record best.
- **Test: Broad jump [battery]** (8): Toes behind the line, arm swing, jump, must stick the landing to count. 3 trials. Measure to the back heel. Record best.
- **Test: Single-leg hop [battery]** (8): Each leg, 2 trials, stick the landing on the same leg. Record both.
- **Test: Line-touch [battery]** (8): Cones at 0 and 5m. Sprint out, touch, back, touch, out. 2 trials. Record best.
- **New Thing** (12): Ready position and split step. Feet wider than shoulders, knees soft, hands ready. Dad claps, Teddy does a tiny hop-and-land, then points where Dad points. 15 reps. Land ready.
- **Soccer: Pass [battery]** (12): Inside-foot passing to a cone gate at 3m, 20 each foot. Then Test: soccer wall passes, 30 seconds alternating feet, count controlled returns. Record.
- **Strong** (8): Bear walk 2×15m, crab walk 2×10m, 2 dead hangs.
- **Challenge [challenge]** (8): Beat Dad's Broad Jump. Dad jumps from kneeling. Teddy tries to beat the mark from standing. Best of 3.
- **Play** (25): Soccer field. Roll-out sharks and minnows: Dad is the stationary shark, rolling balls to tag. Then free kicking at a goal.
- **Shake Out** (5): Walk a lap, calves on the wall, Champion's Log line.

Dad notes: High-intent efforts today: 3 sprints, 3 jumps, 4 hops, 2 shuttles, 3 challenge jumps, about 15. That is the week's load spike, by design. The soccer passing is technical volume and does not count. If he is flat on the third sprint, take the best of two and move on.

### Thu 2026-09-17 · Wall Day · Wall & Ball (110–120 min, high-intent efforts: 2)

- **Wake Up** (10): Animal medley, skips, then 10 shadow forehands and 10 shadow backhands with a full unit turn.
- **New Thing** (15): Cartwheel step 2: hand, hand, foot, foot. Chalk a line. Lunge, reach hand then hand onto the line, kick one foot over then the other, land on the line. 8 each side. Then 10 donkey kicks against the wall.
- **Throw** (10): Step-and-throw at a big chalk square on the tennis wall. Opposite foot steps forward. 15 throws dominant, 10 non-dominant. Score the hits.
- **Tennis [battery]** (30): Drop-feed forehands ×20: unit turn, contact in front, finish over the shoulder. Drop-feed backhands ×20. Wall rally: how many in a row? Then Test: rally count with Dad, cooperative, green ball, best of 3. Record. Introduce the between-point reset: breathe, bounce, look at the strings.
- **Basketball: Footwork & Pass [battery]** (20): Jump stop from a jog, then pivot on each foot, 5 each. Triple-threat stance. Chest passes to a chalk square at 3m ×15, bounce passes ×15. Then Test: cone weave, 5 cones 2m apart, right hand down, left hand back. 2 trials. Record.
- **Strong** (6): 2 dead hangs, hollow hold 2×15s.
- **Challenge [challenge]** (6): Wall Rally Streak. Longest unbroken streak against the wall. Record.
- **Play** (15): Basketball. Dribble tag with Dad stationary and a ball to roll, then HORSE.
- **Shake Out** (5): Champion's Log line.

Dad notes: Form over volume on the tennis. If the finish starts dropping, stop the set at 10, not 20. The basketball cone weave will be slow with the left hand; record it without comment. High-intent efforts stay near zero today.

### Fri 2026-09-18 · Skate Day · Skate & Stick (100–120 min, high-intent efforts: 5)

- **Wake Up** (10): Animal medley, skips, arm circles.
- **Skate park** (30): Free skate. One 5-minute safe-fall lesson: bend the knees, roll onto the side and forearm, never straight arms. Practice from kneeling on grass ×5.
- **New Thing** (12): Single-leg stick. Small hop forward, land on one leg, statue for 3 seconds. 5 each leg. Then hop sideways ×5 each.
- **Soccer: Keeper** (15): On grass. Ready position: knees bent, hands in front, weight forward. Dad hand-feeds 20 balls at chest height for the W-catch (thumbs together behind the ball), then 20 rolling balls for the scoop. No diving yet. Hands first, then hug it.
- **Coordination** (8): Jump rope, 2 rounds of 2 minutes. Tiny hops, wrists not arms.
- **Challenge [challenge]** (7): Silent Landings, round 2. 10 jumps off Monday's step. Beat Monday's number.
- **Play** (25): Soccer field kickaround and passing games. No sprint races today.
- **Shake Out** (5): Calves, hamstrings. Champion's Log: what was your best thing this week?

Dad notes: Saturday is roughly 5 hours of organized sport. Today is balance and skill, high-intent efforts 5 or fewer. Keeper work is catching only this block; diving starts from kneeling in the Fox block once the safe-fall habit is in. Early bed, big dinner, water bottle ready for tomorrow.

### Sat 2026-09-19 · Game Day · Soccer · Lacrosse · Tennis (home off, high-intent efforts: 0)
