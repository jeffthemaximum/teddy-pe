# Cub block · Week 4 (Oct 5 – Oct 11)

## Week 4: Upside Down (Oct 5–11)

Sub-targets: Wall handstand hold, 5 seconds; Cartwheel step 4: straighter legs from the lunge entry; Rings inversion with Dad spotting; Tennis: forehand finish over the shoulder, 10 of 10; Basketball: figure 8 through the legs, eyes up, 10 each way; Soccer: sole rolls and pull-backs, both feet

Challenge of the week: Hang Time. Dead hang, best of 2, in seconds. Monday number, Friday number.

High-intent efforts: 37 of 40.

### Mon 2026-10-05 · Floor Day · Feet in the Sky (60–90 min, high-intent efforts: 0)

- **Wake Up** (8): Animal medley, skips, arm circles. Then wrist circles both ways and 10 palm presses into the floor, because hands carry him today.
- **Challenge [challenge]** (6): Hang Time. Dead hang from the bar, arms straight, chin level. Two tries with a full rest between, and the best one is Monday's number. Again Friday.
- **New Thing** (15): Cartwheel step 4: straighter legs. Same lunge entry as last week, and this time the legs stay long as they pass over the top. 10 each side, Dad scores each one 1 to 3 for straight legs. Scissors in the sky.
- **Upside Down** (10): Donkey kicks ×10, a little higher each time. Then wall handstand, 3 holds, counting out loud. Target is 5 seconds. Walk the feet down if the back starts to bend. Straight as a pencil.
- **Basketball: Handle** (20): Figure 8 through the legs, 10 each way, eyes on Dad's fingers the whole time (he holds up numbers, Teddy calls them). Then crossovers ×20 eyes up, pound dribble 25 each hand. About 200 dribbles.
- **Basketball: Handles, no dribble** (10): Touch roll, 30 steady and 10 fast. Figure 8 roll, 5 times round. Ball glide roll, 10m out and 10m back. Then pass through, 2 × 10m. Fingertips, not palms.
- **Champion's Log** (4): One line: what does the world look like upside down?
- **Play** (10): Skateboard cruise or bike. Nothing timed.

Dad notes: Hang Time goes first, before anything else has tired his grip: two hangs, full rest between, best one counts and goes on the chart. Gymnastics has already loaded him, so the upside down work is short and the handstand walks down the moment his back arches or his elbows bend. On the cartwheel, score the legs only, 1 to 3 for straight. If height is still empty on the Tests tab, measure him today, shoes off against a wall.

### Tue 2026-10-06 · Rings Day · Upside Down on the Rings (75–90 min, high-intent efforts: 6)

- **Wake Up** (10): Skips, arm circles, inchworms ×5, wrist circles.
- **Rings** (20): Riverside traveling rings. 5 passes out, turn, and come back. Dad spots the hips and never pulls. Then the rings inversion on a ring or bar he can reach from the ground: tuck the knees to the chest, lift the hips over the head until he is upside down in a ball, hold 2 seconds, come back down the same way. 3 to 5 reps. Tuck, turn, look at your toes.
- **Strong** (12): Hanging knee tucks ×8, hollow hold 2×20s. Bear walk 2×15m, crab walk 2×10m.
- **Soccer: Touch** (20): Receive and return first, Dad kneeling 3m away and feeding along the ground. 10 leading with each foot at the level that foot is on. Then sole rolls across the body, 20 each foot. Pull-back and go, 10 each foot: step on it, drag it back, turn and take it the other way. Toe taps 50 and tick-tocks 50 to finish. About 300 touches. Step on it and drag it home.
- **Balance** (6): Single-leg balance 30s each leg, then eyes closed for as long as it holds. Record both legs.
- **Play** (8): Wall ball or HORSE, his pick.
- **Shake Out** (4): Champion's Log line.

Dad notes: The inversion is new and you spot every rep: one hand on his back, one on his hips, and he lowers out of it as slowly as he went up. He tucks into a ball and turns over, and that is the whole skill for now; he does not push through to hang behind the bar. The rings passes are the only maximal efforts, about 6. In the soccer block, the pull-back is a drag with the sole, so stop him if he starts poking it backward with his toe.

### Wed 2026-10-07 · Fast Day · Fast Feet, Upside Down (100–120 min, high-intent efforts: 22)

- **Wake Up** (14): A-march, skips, gallops each lead, high knees. Then 3 build-up runs over 20m at 60, 70, 80%.
- **New Thing** (12): Cartwheel step 4, 8 each side, legs long over the top. Then wall handstand, 2 holds.
- **Fast** (14): Reaction starts, 6 × 10m. Athletic stance at the line, go on Dad's clap, and stop in 3 quiet steps at his cone. Walk back slowly between each one. Clap, go, freeze.
- **Power** (10): Stick landing off the 30cm box ×6, stepping off rather than jumping. Single-leg hop and stick, 3 each leg, statue for 3 seconds. Broad jump ×3, sticking every landing.
- **Tennis footwork** (10): Split step into a shuffle. Dad claps, Teddy hops and lands ready, shuffles to where Dad points, then recovers to the middle. 10 of 10.
- **Soccer: Touch at speed** (18): Dribble at speed through cone gates. On Dad's back, pull-back and go the other way. 10 calls on each foot. Then receive and return at the highest level he owns, 10 each foot, with Dad feeding firmer.
- **Play** (25): Soccer game with Dad in goal, or free kicking at a goal.
- **Shake Out** (5): Walk a lap, calves on the wall. Champion's Log line.

Dad notes: High-intent efforts: 6 clap starts over 10m, 6 single-leg hops, 3 broad jumps and about 7 bursts in the soccer game, so 22. Full rest between starts, and you stay at the cone, so you clap and he comes to you. Every landing is a stick; one that wobbles is repeated, and two in a row means the jumping is over for today. The cartwheel and handstand go before the speed work so they get his best attention.

### Thu 2026-10-08 · Wall Day · Finish High (100–120 min, high-intent efforts: 6)

- **Wake Up** (10): Animal medley, skips. 10 shadow forehands with the finish held over the shoulder, 10 shadow backhands.
- **New Thing** (14): Cartwheel step 4, 10 each side, and Dad scores the legs again. Compare with Monday. Then wall handstand, 3 holds, and the best time goes in the diary.
- **Throw** (10): Step-and-throw at the chalk square, 10 right arm, 10 left, moving the line in for the left if the form goes.
- **Tennis** (32): Drop feed forehands in sets of 10, every one with a finish over the shoulder that stays there for a beat. A set counts only if all 10 do. Then backhands ×20, unit turn first. Then rally with Dad, green ball, target 20 in a row. Reset between points. Catch the racket over your shoulder.
- **Basketball: Handle & Pass** (20): Figure 8 through the legs, 10 each way, eyes up, and compare with Monday. Jump stop off a jog, then pivot, 5 on each foot. Chest pass to the 3m target ×10, bounce pass ×10.
- **Basketball: Handles, no dribble** (5): Pass through, 2 × 10m. Then the drop-and-catch pair, walking: spider move, 10 catches, and clap tap, 10 catches. Count the catches and compare with last Sunday. Drop, step, snatch.
- **Strong** (6): Hollow hold 2×20s, bear walk 2×15m.
- **Play** (14): Basketball. HORSE and dribble tag with Dad stationary.
- **Shake Out** (5): Champion's Log line.

Dad notes: The tennis target is 10 forehands in a row that finish over the shoulder and hold there for a beat, so count only the held ones and start the count again after a miss. Keep the feeds easy so all of his attention goes to the finish. No dead hangs today, so his grip is fresh for Friday's Hang Time. High-intent efforts are the play block, about 6.
