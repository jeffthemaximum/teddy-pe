## Daily cards, Week 1: Baseline & Land (Sep 14–20)

### Mon Sep 14 · Floor Day · Land Like a Cat (45 min)

- **8 min · Wake Up**: Animal walk medley: bear, crab, monkey, flamingo, giraffe, 10m each. Then 10 skips.
- **12 min · New Thing**: Stick landing from the ground. Jump up, land in cat position: feet quiet, knees over toes, chest up, hands out front. Freeze 3 seconds. 10 reps, Dad scores each 1 to 3 for quiet. Then 10 from a curb or bottom step.
- **10 min · Cartwheel step 1**: Bunny hops over a chalk line. Hands on the line, kick both feet over to the other side, land feet together. 10 each direction. Arms straight, look at your hands.
- **7 min · Challenge [challenge]**: Silent Landings. 10 jumps off the step. Count how many make no sound. Write the number on the chart. Again Friday.
- **10 min · Play**: Skateboard cruise or bike. Nothing timed.

Dad notes: Gym already loaded him. Zero sprinting and zero jumping for distance today. Watch the knees on landing: if they cave inward, lower the step. Step no higher than his knee.

### Tue Sep 15 · Rings Day · Test Day 1: Hands & Arms (60–75 min)

- **10 min · Wake Up**: Skips, big and small arm circles, bear crawl 2×10m, inchworms ×5.
- **5 min · Test: Dead hang [battery]**: Best of 2, 2 min rest between. Straight arms, thumbs around the bar. Record seconds.
- **10 min · Test: Overhand throw [battery]**: Tennis ball, from a step. 3 throws right, 3 throws left. Mark the best with a cone, pace it. Record both arms.
- **8 min · Test: Jump rope [battery]**: Consecutive singles without a miss. Best of 3. Record.
- **5 min · Single-leg balance**: Each leg, eyes open, arms out, up to 60s. Record each leg (skill unlock, not a battery item).
- **15 min · Rings**: Riverside traveling rings. Hang and swing ×5. Then reach for the next ring with Dad spotting the hips. Goal: 3 passes. If he gets 3, try for 5.
- **15 min · Play**: Basketball court. HORSE, Dad shoots from one spot.

Dad notes: Tests before rings, while he is fresh. Full rest between throws; these are max efforts. Cheer the effort, record the number without commentary. The non-dominant arm will look bad. That is the point: it is the one that will move most this year. Swim class after is recovery, add nothing.

### Wed Sep 16 · Fast Day · Test Day 2: Fast & Far (100–120 min)

- **12 min · Wake Up**: A-march 2×15m, skips 2×15m, gallops each lead 2×15m, high knees 2×10m. Then 3 build-up runs over 20m at 60, 70, 80%.
- **10 min · Test: 20m sprint [battery]**: Standing start behind a line, Dad at 20m with the phone timer: ready, set, go. 3 trials, walk-back rest of 2 min. Record best.
- **8 min · Test: Broad jump [battery]**: Toes behind the line, arm swing, jump, must stick the landing to count. 3 trials. Measure to the back heel. Record best.
- **8 min · Test: Single-leg hop [battery]**: Each leg, 2 trials, stick the landing on the same leg. Record both.
- **8 min · Test: Line-touch [battery]**: Cones at 0 and 5m. Sprint out, touch, back, touch, out. 2 trials. Record best.
- **12 min · New Thing**: Ready position and split step. Feet wider than shoulders, knees soft, hands ready. Dad claps, Teddy does a tiny hop-and-land, then points where Dad points. 15 reps. Land ready.
- **8 min · Strong**: Bear walk 2×15m, crab walk 2×10m, 2 dead hangs.
- **8 min · Challenge [challenge]**: Beat Dad's Broad Jump. Dad jumps from kneeling. Teddy tries to beat the mark from standing. Best of 3.
- **30 min · Play**: Soccer field. Roll-out sharks and minnows: Dad is the stationary shark, rolling balls to tag. Then free kicking.
- **5 min · Shake Out**: Walk a lap, calves on the wall, one thing he did well.

Dad notes: High-intent efforts today: 3 sprints, 3 jumps, 4 hops, 2 shuttles, 3 challenge jumps, about 15. That is the week's load spike, by design. If he is flat on the third sprint, take the best of two and move on.

### Thu Sep 17 · Wall Day · Wall & Ball (100–120 min)

- **10 min · Wake Up**: Animal medley, skips, then 10 shadow forehands and 10 shadow backhands with a full unit turn.
- **15 min · New Thing**: Cartwheel step 2: hand, hand, foot, foot. Chalk a line. Lunge, reach hand then hand onto the line, kick one foot over then the other, land on the line. 8 each side. Then 10 donkey kicks against the wall.
- **12 min · Throw**: Step-and-throw at a big chalk square on the tennis wall. Opposite foot steps forward. 15 throws dominant, 10 non-dominant. Score the hits.
- **30 min · Tennis [battery]**: Drop-feed forehands ×20: unit turn, contact in front, finish over the shoulder. Drop-feed backhands ×20. Wall rally: how many in a row? Then Test: rally count with Dad, cooperative, current ball, best of 3. Record.
- **8 min · Strong**: 2 dead hangs, hollow hold 2×15s.
- **6 min · Challenge [challenge]**: Wall Rally Streak. Longest unbroken streak against the wall. Record.
- **25 min · Play**: Basketball. Dribble tag with Dad stationary and a ball to roll, then HORSE.
- **5 min · Shake Out**

Dad notes: Form over volume. If the finish starts dropping, stop the set at 10, not 20. Tennis is cooperative today so high-intent efforts stay near zero. Keep them there.

### Fri Sep 18 · Skate Day · Skate & Stick (90–120 min)

- **10 min · Wake Up**: Animal medley, skips, arm circles.
- **30 min · Skate park**: Free skate. One 5-minute safe-fall lesson: bend the knees, roll onto the side and forearm, never straight arms. Practice from kneeling on grass ×5.
- **12 min · New Thing**: Single-leg stick. Small hop forward, land on one leg, statue for 3 seconds. 5 each leg. Then hop sideways ×5 each.
- **8 min · Coordination**: Jump rope, 2 rounds of 2 minutes. Tiny hops, wrists not arms.
- **7 min · Challenge [challenge]**: Silent Landings, round 2. 10 jumps off Monday's step. Beat Monday's number.
- **30 min · Play**: Soccer field kickaround and passing games. No sprint races today.
- **5 min · Shake Out**: Calves, hamstrings. Ask: what was your best thing this week?

Dad notes: Saturday is roughly 5 hours of organized sport. Today is balance and skill, high-intent efforts 5 or fewer. Early bed, big dinner, water bottle ready for tomorrow.
